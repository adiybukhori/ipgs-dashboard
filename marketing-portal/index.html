Matching Rules

1. Normalise and compare exact IC / Passport. No automatic name-only or email-only matches.
2. Count each person once across both marketing sheets.
3. Group by the preferred intake recorded in Marketing Registration.
4. A Snapshot Active status confirms activation, even where ACC status is outdated.
5. Never write student status changes back to ACC from the marketing portal.
